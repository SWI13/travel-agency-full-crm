Practice safely

Ask your administrator to switch on sample data. Sample customers, packages and bookings carry a DEMO badge. You cannot harm anything real while you practise, and nobody is contacted.

One thing to remember from the very start

The system shows you customers and reminds you to act. It does not send WhatsApp messages. You always reply from the WhatsApp Business app on the agency phone (or press Open WhatsApp in the system). The system will also not create a reminder for you — you create it. Lesson 7 explains this fully.

Start here

1The big idea in 2 minutes

Think of the system as a notebook that fills itself. Every person who writes to YourTour gets one page in the notebook. That page is called a lead.

1Customer writesOn WhatsApp or the website form.
2A page appearsName, number and message are filled in for you.
3You answerFrom the WhatsApp Business app.
4You write notesTrip wishes, budget, what was agreed.
5You set a reminderSo the customer is never forgotten.

Five words to learn

LeadOne customer page. One page per phone number. Everything about the customer is on it.
StageWhere the customer is: just arrived, in talks, confirmed, travelled… Like a train line with stations.
Follow-upA reminder you set: "contact this customer on this day at this hour".
PackageA trip we sell (for example Umrah 15 days).
BookingA customer who said yes and reserved places.
Lesson 1 · 3 minutes

2Open the system and sign in

  1. Open the browser (Chrome) on the agency computer.
  2. Type the system address and add /admin at the end. Today it is http://localhost:5173/admin. After the system goes online it will be your own web address. Save it as a bookmark.
  3. Type your email and your password. Your administrator gave them to you.
  4. Press Sign in.
  5. First time only: click your name (top right) → Change password. Choose a password only you know, at least 10 characters.
Choose your language

Click the little translate icon (文A) at the top. Pick English, Français or العربية. In Arabic the whole screen turns right-to-left.

Never share your password

Everything you do is recorded with your name. If someone else uses your account, their mistakes are written under your name. If you forget your password, ask the administrator to reset it.

Exercise 1
  • Sign in.
  • Change your password.
  • Switch the language to French, then back.
  • Sign out (name → Sign out) and sign in again.
Lesson 2 · 5 minutes

3Know your screen

You only need to know three areas:

1 · The menu (left, dark green)Your pages. The pages you use most are WhatsApp Leads and Follow-ups. A number on WhatsApp Leads = customers with messages you have not read. A red number on Follow-ups = reminders that are late.
2 · The top barSearch (find a customer by name or number), the speaker (sound on/off), the bell (what is new), and your name.
3 · The middleThe page you opened. The Overview shows 8 numbers. Click any number to see the customers behind it.
The green light

At the bottom of the menu you see Live with a green dot. Green = new customers appear by themselves, without refreshing. If you see Reconnecting… (orange), check the internet.

Exercise 2
  • Click each item in the menu once and look at it.
  • Click the speaker icon: do you see it change?
  • Use Search to find a customer by typing two letters.
Lesson 3 · 5 minutes

4A customer arrives — the lead

Keep the dashboard open during work. When a customer writes on WhatsApp, three things happen by themselves.

  1. A new card appears at the top of the first column, New.
  2. You hear a chime (if the sound is on) and see a pop-up with an Open button.
  3. The tab title of the browser shows a number, for example (3), even if you are on another tab.

How to read the card

Name and numberFilled in for you from WhatsApp. If the customer has no profile name you see only the number.
Green number + NEW MESSAGEUnread messages. Green border = needs an answer.
Message textThe last thing written.
Small tagsUmrah or Hajj, and where the customer came from (a Facebook ad, the website, or "Organic WhatsApp" = they just wrote to us).
"Unassigned"No employee owns this customer yet. A manager gives it to someone (Lesson 5).

Lesson 4 · 5 minutes

5Answer the customer

Speed matters. A customer who waits an hour often writes to another agency.

  1. Click the card. The panel opens.
  2. Click the tab Conversation to read the messages. This also marks them as read.
  3. Press the green button Open WhatsApp. The chat opens (WhatsApp Web or the phone).
  4. Write your reply there — not in the system.
  5. Come back to the system for the next lesson: save what you learned.
Why can't I reply inside the system?

The agency keeps using the WhatsApp Business app. The system keeps a copy of the conversation so nothing is lost and the whole team sees it. Replies you send from the app appear in the Conversation tab too, once WhatsApp is connected.

Who owns the customer?

If the card says Unassigned: a manager opens the card and chooses an employee in Assigned agent (bottom of the first tab), then presses Save changes. An agent sees only customers assigned to them. If your list is empty, nobody has assigned you customers yet — ask your manager.

Lesson 5 · 5 minutes

6Save what you learn

After you talked to the customer, write down the important things on their page. Next week you will not remember; the page will.

  1. Open the customer. Click the first tab, Customer.
  2. Display name — type the name you want to use (for example "Mme Benali"). WhatsApp will never overwrite it.
  3. Travel interest — choose Umrah or Hajj, the package they like, dates, number of adults and children, and the budget.
  4. Scroll down to Sales management: choose the stage (Lesson 6) and write notes: "wants December, 4 people, asked about payment in two parts".
  5. Press Save changes at the bottom right. The bar at the bottom says Unsaved changes until you do.
Do not type passport numbers or ID numbers

Not in notes, not anywhere. Write only "passport received" or "photos missing". Keep the original documents in the agency's secure file.

Exercise 4

Open a sample customer. Set a display name, choose Umrah, set 2 adults, write a note, and press Save changes. Close the panel and open it again: is everything still there?

7Stages: where is the customer?

Every customer is at one stage. Moving the card to the next stage shows the whole team how far the customer has come.

QualifiedIt is a real project: dates and people known.Choose a package for them.

Three ways to move a card

  • Drag the card with the mouse into another column.
  • Click the small ⇄ icon at the bottom right of the card and choose the stage (easiest on a phone).
  • Change Lead stage in the customer's panel and press Save changes.
What moves by itself

Only one thing: when the agency replies from the WhatsApp Business app to a customer at New, the card goes to Contacted and the unread number clears. Everything else is your decision.

Lesson 7 · the most important lesson

8Follow-up and call back: when, and how

A follow-up is a reminder that YOU create. The system never creates one by itself.

A follow-up says: "on this day, at this time, contact this customer, for this reason." The system then puts it in your list, turns it red when it is late, and counts it on the Overview.

When does a customer become a follow-up?

Ask yourself one question at the end of every contact with a customer:

"What is the next step, and is it later than today?" → If yes, set a follow-up before you close the panel.
SituationReason to chooseWhen to set it
The customer says "call me tomorrow / next week"Call backThe exact day and hour they asked.
You sent a package and wait for the answerSend offerIn 1–2 days. Ask "did you see the offer?"
The customer said yes, papers are missingDocumentsIn 2–3 days, or before the deadline.
You wait for the depositDepositOn the day they promised to pay.
The customer is "thinking about it"Other (note: "thinking")In 3–5 days.
The customer does not answerCall backTry again the next day, then in 3 days. After that, move to Lost with the reason "no answer".
The customer is ready and everything is done—No follow-up needed. Create the booking.
A customer without a follow-up can be forgotten

If a customer is in a stage like Contacted or Package sent and has no reminder, nobody will remind you. That is exactly how customers are lost. A card with a follow-up date shows a small calendar at its bottom. No calendar = no safety net.

"Call back" — three different meanings

MeaningWhat the system does by itselfWhat you do
1. The customer asks to be called (call-back form on the website)Creates a lead with source Website form, with their name, number and message. Rings the chime. It does not create a follow-up.Call them as soon as you can. If you cannot reach them, set a follow-up (reason Call back).
2. The customer says "call me later" (on WhatsApp or phone)Nothing. The system cannot understand the message.Set a follow-up for the time they gave, reason Call back.
3. You promise to call backNothing.Set a follow-up so you do not forget your own promise.

The same logic applies to every kind of reminder: the system keeps your promises only if you write them down as a follow-up.

How to set a follow-up — step by step

  1. Open the customer (click the card).
  2. Click the third tab, Follow-ups · Bookings.
  3. Under Schedule a follow-up, choose the date and time. The system proposes tomorrow at 10:00 — change it if needed.
  4. Choose the reason: Call back, Send offer, Documents, Deposit or Other.
  5. Write a short note for your future self: "Ask if she saw the 15-day offer".
  6. Press Schedule. The reminder appears in the list above the form.
What happens next

The reminder is assigned to the customer's agent. On the card, the next date appears next to a small calendar. On the due day it shows in Follow-ups under Today. If you miss it, it turns red under Overdue, the menu shows a red number, and the Overview counts the customer under Awaiting follow-up.

When does a follow-up end?

  • You did it: press Mark completed. (Then set the next follow-up if the customer is not finished!)
  • The date changed: press Reschedule and choose Tomorrow, In 3 days, Next week, or an exact date.
  • You pressed it by mistake: press Reopen.
  • The customer is Confirmed or Lost: complete or leave the old reminders; they no longer matter.
The chain

A sale is a chain of follow-ups: contact → follow-up → offer → follow-up → documents → follow-up → deposit → booking. Every time you finish one link, create the next one.

Lesson 8 · 4 minutes

9Your follow-up list every day

Open Follow-ups in the menu. You see four groups:

OverdueThe time has passed. Do these first. They sit in a red frame.
TodayDue today. Go through them from the top.
UpcomingLater. Nothing to do yet.
CompletedClick the heading to see what you have done.

For each reminder

  1. Click the customer's name to open their page and read your note.
  2. Contact them (Open WhatsApp or Call).
  3. Write what happened in the notes. Move the stage if needed.
  4. Press Mark completed — and set the next follow-up.
Goal for the end of every day

Overdue = 0. Every open customer has a future follow-up date.

Lesson 9 · 6 minutes

10The customer says yes: booking

  1. Move the customer to Confirmed when the deposit is received. Until then the booking button stays grey.
  2. Open the customer → tab Follow-ups · Bookings → Convert to booking.
  3. Choose the departure (date). Only departures with free places are listed.
  4. Enter the number of travellers and the booking date.
  5. Check the total price (the system suggests it; change it only if you agreed another price).
  6. Type what the customer has already paid. The remaining balance shows below.
  7. Press Create booking.

The system now saves the booking, creates one traveller record per person, and takes the places from the departure (the website shows the new number of places left).

Exercise 6

Move a sample customer to Confirmed, then create a booking for 2 travellers with part of the price paid. Open Pilgrims & Bookings: do you see it?

Lesson 10 · 5 minutes

11Payments and travel documents

Record a payment

  1. Menu → Pilgrims & Bookings → tab Bookings.
  2. Click the pencil at the end of the booking's row.
  3. Change Amount paid to the new total received (not just the new payment). Keep Payment on Automatic.
  4. Press Save changes. The label becomes Unpaid, Partially paid or Paid by itself.

Track the documents

  1. Same page, tab Pilgrims. One line per traveller.
  2. Click each step when you receive it: passport, photos, documents. It turns green with a tick.
  3. Click the visa label to change it: not started → in progress → submitted → approved.
  4. The bar fills up and says Ready when everything is complete.

You can click a traveller's name to type the real name (the system starts with the customer's name plus 1, 2, 3…).

12Customers from the website

Customers can also come from the website, in two ways. Both end up as a lead in WhatsApp Leads.

How they cameWhat you seeWhat you do
They pressed a WhatsApp button on a packageSource Website → WhatsApp. Their first message starts with a code like [OMR-01]. The package is already chosen for you.Reply on WhatsApp like any customer.
They filled in the call-back formSource Website form. The message shows what they typed (adults, children, question).Call them. That is what they asked for. Then follow Lesson 7.
The same customer, twice

If the same phone number writes again (on WhatsApp or the form), the system adds it to the same page. You never get two pages for one number. Everything you typed stays.

Put it together

13Your day in 10 steps

  1. Sign in and keep the dashboard open in a tab. Check the sound is on.
  2. Open Follow-ups. Do every Overdue one, then Today.
  3. Open WhatsApp Leads. Find cards with a green number.
  4. For each one: read the Conversation, press Open WhatsApp, reply.
  5. Come back: fill in the details, add a note, choose the stage, Save changes.
  6. Set the next follow-up for every customer who is not finished.
  7. When a customer pays the deposit: Confirmed → booking.
  8. Record payments received today. Tick documents received today.
  9. Check the end-of-day goals: Overdue = 0, no unread green numbers, no open customer without a follow-up date.
  10. Sign out on a shared computer.

15Quick card and mini quiz

The 5 rules
  1. Answer fast, from the WhatsApp Business app.
  2. Write notes on the customer's page, then Save.
  3. Keep the stage up to date.
  4. Every open customer needs a follow-up date.
  5. Overdue = 0 at the end of the day.
Where is…
  • New customers → WhatsApp Leads
  • My reminders → Follow-ups
  • Payments and documents → Pilgrims & Bookings
  • Trips and prices → Packages, Departures
  • Who did what → Activity Logs
