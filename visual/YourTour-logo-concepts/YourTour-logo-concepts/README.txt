YourTour Travel — logo concepts (round 2: a plane that makes a tour)

D-Loop        Plane leaves Batna (dotted start), flies a full loop, heads out.
E-Orbit       Plane circles the Y and is about to land back at its starting dot.
F-Round-trip  A Y made of two routes: solid outbound flight, dotted way home.

Each folder has:
  *-mark                      symbol only, transparent background
  *-mark-one-colour           symbol in black (stamps, fax, receipts, engraving)
  *-logo-stacked              symbol + names, centred (transparent)
  *-logo-stacked-reversed     same on petrol background
  *-logo-horizontal           symbol left, names right (transparent)
  *-logo-horizontal-reversed  same on petrol background
  *-profile-picture-1080      square for WhatsApp / Instagram / Facebook (they crop it to a circle)

SVG = for printers, sign makers and the website (scales to any size).
PNG = for phones, posts and WhatsApp.
Text is converted to shapes, so the files look the same on every device.

Colours   Aurès Petrol #0D3B44 · Saffron #F2A93B · Timgad Stone #F5EEE3 · Ink #14181C
Typeface  Alexandria (Google Fonts, free; covers Arabic and Latin)

These are concepts for choosing a direction, not the final identity.
